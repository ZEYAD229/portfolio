About

Mediterranean Soul. Global Perspective.

Zeyad Elsaid is an Egyptian multidisciplinary professional working across event management, public relations, marketing, media and communications. His journey began in 2016 through student activities and the Egyptian Schools Student Union Council.

Since then he has helped organise and manage conferences, festivals, exhibitions, corporate events, educational programmes and large-scale activations across Egypt, spanning healthcare, banking, tourism, culture, education and entertainment.

He is currently Senior Events Manager at Almehwar Hospital, is developing his own marketing agency Impactra, and is a Master’s researcher in Public Relations and Advertising at Cairo University.

Born by the Mediterranean. Inspired by the World.Alexandria shapes his outlook, artistic interests and communication style.
