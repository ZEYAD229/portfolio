Zeyad Elsaid is an Egyptian multidisciplinary professional working across event management, public relations, marketing, media and communications.

His professional journey began in 2016 through student activities and the Egyptian Schools Student Union Council. Since then, he has contributed to conferences, festivals, exhibitions, corporate events, educational programmes and activations across Egypt, spanning healthcare, banking, tourism, culture and education.

He is currently a Senior Events Specialist at AlMehwar Hospital, developing Impactra as a full-service marketing agency, and pursuing postgraduate studies in Public Relations and Advertising at Cairo University.
